## Typography

Open Sauce One is the primary face. Localized files are in the page font namespace at weights 400, 600, and 700. Geist 600 appears only as a supporting face in a handful of utility labels.

## Geometry

Desktop content is inset 20px on a 1440px viewport and uses a 1385px effective maximum width. The navigation and most cards use fully rounded pill corners; large editorial cards use approximately 24–32px radii. Desktop display sections frequently lock to 900px. Compact layouts switch at 768px and stack into a 16px horizontal gutter.

## Motion

Primary timing is physical and unhurried: 350ms control transitions, roughly 700ms content reveals, and approximately 1500ms FAQ crossfades. Scroll effects use sticky layers rather than translating the entire document. Marquees are linear and infinite.
